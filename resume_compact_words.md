# Compact Resume Words & Phrases

## Action Verbs by Category

### Leadership & Management
- Led, Directed, Managed, Supervised, Coordinated
- Spearheaded, Orchestrated, Guided, Mentored, Delegated
- Pioneered, Initiated, Launched, Established, Founded

### Achievement & Results
- Achieved, Delivered, Exceeded, Accomplished, Attained
- Boosted, Increased, Enhanced, Improved, Optimized
- Generated, Produced, Created, Built, Developed

### Problem Solving
- Resolved, Troubleshot, Diagnosed, Analyzed, Investigated
- Streamlined, Simplified, Automated, Refined, Restructured
- Innovated, Designed, Engineered, Architected, Crafted

### Communication & Collaboration
- Collaborated, Partnered, Liaised, Facilitated, Negotiated
- Presented, Communicated, Advised, Consulted, Influenced
- Trained, Educated, Coached, Mentored, Onboarded

## Quantifiable Impact Words
- **Percentages**: "Increased by 25%", "Reduced costs by 30%"
- **Numbers**: "Managed team of 15", "Processed 500+ requests daily"
- **Time**: "Delivered ahead of schedule", "Reduced processing time by 2 hours"
- **Money**: "Saved $50K annually", "Generated $1M in revenue"

## Technical Skills (Compact)
### Programming
- **Languages**: Python, Java, JavaScript, C++, SQL, HTML/CSS
- **Frameworks**: React, Node.js, Django, Spring, Angular
- **Databases**: MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis
- **Cloud**: AWS, Azure, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes

### Tools & Software
- **Design**: Figma, Adobe Suite, Sketch, Canva
- **Analytics**: Excel, Tableau, Power BI, Google Analytics
- **Project Management**: Jira, Trello, Asana, Slack
- **Version Control**: Git, GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket

## Professional Summary Templates (2-3 lines)

### Software Developer
"Full-stack developer with 5+ years building scalable web applications. Proficient in React, Node.js, and AWS. Delivered 20+ projects, reducing development time by 40%."

### Marketing Professional
"Digital marketing specialist driving 300% growth in online engagement. Expert in SEO, SEM, and social media campaigns. Managed $500K+ advertising budgets across multiple platforms."

### Project Manager
"Certified PMP with 7+ years leading cross-functional teams. Delivered 50+ projects on time and under budget. Specialized in Agile methodologies and stakeholder management."

### Data Analyst
"Data-driven analyst extracting insights from complex datasets. Proficient in Python, SQL, and Tableau. Automated reporting processes, saving 15 hours weekly."

## Experience Section Format
**Job Title** | **Company** | **Dates**
• Achievement with quantifiable result
• Technical skill application with impact
• Leadership/collaboration example with outcome

### Example:
**Senior Software Engineer** | **TechCorp** | **2021-2024**
• Developed microservices architecture, reducing system downtime by 60%
• Led team of 8 developers using Agile methodology, delivering 15+ features quarterly
• Optimized database queries, improving application response time by 45%

## Skills Section (Compact Format)
**Technical**: Python, React, AWS, Docker, PostgreSQL, Git
**Soft Skills**: Leadership, Problem-solving, Communication, Team Collaboration
**Certifications**: AWS Certified, Scrum Master, Google Analytics Certified

## Education (Minimal Format)
**Degree** | **University** | **Year** | **GPA** (if 3.5+)
*Relevant coursework*: Key subjects related to target role

## Impact Metrics Examples
- Revenue: "Generated $2M in new business"
- Efficiency: "Reduced processing time from 4 hours to 30 minutes"
- Growth: "Grew social media following by 500%"
- Cost Savings: "Cut operational costs by $100K annually"
- Team: "Managed cross-functional team of 12 members"
- Quality: "Achieved 99.9% uptime across all systems"

## Resume Bullet Point Formula
**Action Verb** + **What You Did** + **How You Did It** + **Quantifiable Result**

Example: "Implemented automated testing framework using Selenium, reducing manual testing time by 70% and improving deployment frequency from weekly to daily."

## Common Resume Buzzwords to AVOID
- "Hard worker", "Team player", "Detail-oriented"
- "Responsible for", "Duties included", "Helped with"
- "Excellent communication skills" (show, don't tell)
- "Synergy", "Paradigm", "Think outside the box"

## Compact Formatting Tips
- Use bullet points, not paragraphs
- Start each bullet with an action verb
- Keep bullets to 1-2 lines maximum
- Use consistent formatting throughout
- Include only relevant information
- Quantify everything possible
